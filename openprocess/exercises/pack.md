# OpenProcess demo exercises

Seven short worksheets: Petri nets, soundness, the α-algorithm, regions,
markings and matrices, the Inductive Miner, and conformance checking with a
workflow. Answer in the boxes and press **Check**: most answers are checked
straight away, and the app never shows you a result you are meant to work out
until you ask for it.

Your answers are saved in each exercise's folder (*my answers.json*, and
*my answer.pnml* for a net you draw, *my workflow.analysis* for a workflow
you build), so you can stop and carry on later.

To write your own pack, copy this folder and change the `question.md` files:
see *Help ▸ Writing Exercise Packs*.
